What is SMathHacks?

An annual 36-hour hackathon hosted by the North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics, open to high school students across North Carolina.

Meet new people and build a project together. Talks, hands-on workshops, and mentors will help you explore new technology along the way.

Your first hackathon? You’re welcome here.

    FAQs

    If we haven't answered your question here, please feel free to email smathhacks@ncssm.edu and take a look here!

    Are you allowed to form a team on the day of SMathHacks?

    Yes, you will be able to make teams at the start of the hackathon. If you don't have a team in mind, we'll have a team formation event at the start of SMathHacks!

    See last year's projects!

    Explore the 2026 winners and projects, or browse earlier years in our Past Events archive.

    What is a “track”, and how do I choose one?

    Choose the track that best represents your project’s main goal or experience. Projects may fit multiple categories, but you may submit to only one track.

    What's a hackathon?

    A hackathon is an event where students work in a team, or by themselves, to build an innovative piece of software in a limited amount of time. Students will form teams around a problem or idea, and sprint to create a unique solution—these typically take the form of mobile applications, websites, or other digital solutions, however, hardware projects will also be accepted under specific regulations.

    What if I’ve never programmed before?

    That’s okay! This event is beginner-friendly, meaning that you don’t need any prior programming experience to participate! There will be incredible talks, workshops, and mentors available to guide you through the ideation and development process.

    Where’s the schedule for the event?

    The full 2027 schedule will appear in the schedule section when confirmed. Visit Event essentials for workshop and virtual-session links as they become available.

    How do teams work?

    You can have up to four people in a team. You can list teammates on the interest form or fill it out individually. If you don’t have a team yet, that’s okay—we’ll have a team formation event to help you find people to work with.

    Can I use AI to help me brainstorm?

    Yes, but you cannot use AI to program a primary part of your project. For a more detailed description, see our Use of AI Statement.
